My fascination with trainspotting and street photography began with my first visit to Japan. Since then, I've been drawn to the everyday scenery where manmade structures meet the natural world, from trains winding through the countryside to radio towers woven into the urban landscape.

Inspired by the atmospheric scenes of Japanese slice-of-life anime, I've come to appreciate the beauty of ordinary moments — glimpses of daily life framed by distinctive architecture, picturesque landscapes, and the changing seasons.

This album features selected photos from my trips to Japan.
