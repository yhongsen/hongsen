I’m frequently asked if I ever plan to move back to the East Coast, and I always say I would if the opportunity arose. California’s moderate climate is hard to match, but I often miss the rhythm of four distinct seasons. Having grown up in New England, each change in weather felt like a reminder to slow down and stay grounded in the present. New England has its share of brutally hot and humid summers and frigid winters, but these imperfections are part of what makes it familiar. Looking back, each season carries memories of childhood and a feeling of home.

This collection features photos from Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Maine.
